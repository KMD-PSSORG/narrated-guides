# ProcureFlow 365 · Requesting a stapler

A video guide to the requisition flow in ProcureFlow 365. It also shows how
the guide itself was made, and it introduces four voices: Janet (narrator),
Daniel (Compliance), Bent (final approval) and G. Gourdon (a pumpkin).

`[tags]` set the voice's tone. `{cues}` are actions on screen and are not read
aloud. ```` ```speak compliance ```` and friends switch to another voice.

## Scene 1 · Title

*Screen:* Title card: "Requesting a stapler".

```speak
[calm, dry] Welcome to ProcureFlow three sixty-five. In this guide, you will request a stapler. [pauses] The guide takes about four minutes. The stapler takes somewhat longer.
```

## Scene 4 · How this guide was made

*Screen:* The Behind the scenes page. The four boxes of the pipeline light up in turn.

```speak
[conspiratorially] Before we start, a word about this video. {vis: manuscript} It began as a manuscript, written in plain Markdown. {vis: voice} The voice was generated first, so we know exactly when every word is spoken. {vis: recording} Then a browser recorded the screen, and every click lands on its word. {vis: assembly} Finally, voice and picture were put together, with subtitles.
```

## Scene 6 · Find the item

```speak

[pauses] The red one was discontinued in nineteen ninety-eight. People still ask for it.

## Scene 7 · Choose the cost centre

## Scene 14 · How the cues work

*Screen:* The manuscript of scene 12. The cue in front of "submit" is circled.

```speak
Remember the click on Submit? {ring: submit cue} It happened exactly on the word "submit", because the cue was written right in front of it. [pauses] The voice comes first, and the picture follows. That is the whole trick. [whispers] Don't tell Procurement.

```speak pumpkin
[deep, ominous] Thirty-nine years. [pauses] Thirty-nine years I have waited for one pumpkin. [whispers] I filled in every field. [growls] I wrote the justification. [ominous] And still it says... awaiting approval. [long pause] Approve... my... pumpkin.
```
