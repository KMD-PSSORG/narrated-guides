Narrated decks

Write what the slides should say in the speaker notes. Get the deck back with a voice on every slide, the animations on the right words, and slides that move on by themselves. PowerPoint then makes the video.

Everything happens in this browser. Your deck is not uploaded anywhere, and your ElevenLabs key goes only to ElevenLabs, along with the text from the notes.

1 Your deck

No deck at hand? , a three-phase timeline with one extra voice.

How to write the speaker notes

Write in the notes what the voice should say on that slide. Slides without notes are left as they are.

A requisition goes through three phases.
{vis: Phase 1} First, you find the item. [pauses]
{vis: Phase 2} Then you fill in the details.
{voice: bent} [deadpan] Bent approves. Eventually.
  • {vis: Phase 1} makes the shape called “Phase 1” appear on the next word. {show: …} works too. The name is the one in the Selection Pane (Home → Arrange → Selection Pane), where you can also rename shapes.
  • If the shape already has an animation, that animation is used and moved onto the word. Shapes in the same click (“With Previous” and “After Previous”) come along. A shape without an animation fades in.
  • {voice: bent} lets another voice say the rest of the slide. You pick the voice below.
  • [pauses], [deadpan] and other words in square brackets tell the voice how to say it. They are not read aloud.
Let Copilot in PowerPoint write the notes

The narrate-deck-notes skill teaches Copilot this format. It writes the notes, suggests names for the shapes, and estimates how long the video will be.

  1. In PowerPoint, open Copilot, choose … → Manage skills → Custom skills, and switch custom skills on.
  2. Choose Add skill and upload the SKILL.md file.
  3. Ask Copilot: @narrate-deck-notes write a voice-over for this deck, about two minutes, calm and friendly.

    Advanced

    4 Make it

      Download the narrated deck

      Then, in PowerPoint

      1. Open the deck and press Play from start to watch it run by itself.
      2. File → Export → Create a Video. Choose Use Recorded Timings and Narrations.
      3. Changed a word? Change the notes, drop the narrated deck here again and run it once more. Only slides whose text changed cost characters.
